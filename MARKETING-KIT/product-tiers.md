# Product Tier Strategy — GrabKit

## Tier 1: Free Lead Magnet (3 scripts) — $0
**Purpose:** Drive traffic, collect emails, upsell to paid pack.
**Contents:** File Organizer, Duplicate Finder, Folder Size (the 3 most universally useful)
**Distribution:** GitHub (public repo), Reddit, Dev.to, Hacker News
**CTA:** "Want 9 more tools? Get the full GrabKit for $9.99 → [Gumroad link]"
**Email capture:** Ask for email before download (use Gumroad's free product with email gate, or a simple form)

## Tier 2: GrabKit Core — $9.99 (current product)
**Contents:** All 12 scripts + README + setup guide
**Target:** General consumers, small business owners, students
**Channel:** Gumroad, Etsy, Fiverr gigs

## Tier 3: GrabKit Pro — $29.99 (future, upsell)
**Contents:** All 12 scripts + 5 advanced tools:
- Watch folder (auto-organize new files as they arrive)
- Batch image watermark
- Excel/CSV advanced (pivot, filter, dedup columns)
- Folder sync (one-way mirror between two folders)
- Scheduled backup (cron/Task Scheduler wrapper)
**Target:** Power users, freelancers, small IT teams
**Channel:** Gumroad (upsell from Core purchase email)

## Tier 4: GrabKit Business — $99 (future, B2B)
**Contents:** Pro pack + commercial license + priority email support + custom script (1 request)
**Target:** Small businesses, agencies
**Channel:** Direct outreach, LinkedIn

## Revenue math to $50
- 5 × Core ($9.99) = $49.95 ≈ $50
- OR 2 × Pro ($29.99) = $59.98
- OR 1 × Business ($99) = $99
- OR 10 × $5 tips (Ko-fi) = $50

## Upsell flow
Free (GitHub) → Core ($9.99, Gumroad) → Pro ($29.99, email to buyers) → Business ($99, LinkedIn outreach)
Each tier's buyers get an email offering the next tier at a discount.
