# START HERE — where the money actually comes from

Read this one page. Everything else in this repo is detail.

## The hard truth

The free pack will not sell 5 copies at $9.99. Generic Python scripts are free
everywhere, and Gumroad/Stripe/PayPal payouts to Ukraine are blocked. What sells is
**a result delivered to one person**, in hryvnia, paid to a card today.

So there are two offers, and the order matters:

1. **Service** (fast money, no platform, no KYC): "I will clean up your files / photos / PDFs."
   499 ₴ per job, 299 ₴ per photo pack. Paid to a Monobank jar.
2. **Product** (slower, passive): GrabKit Desktop, 249 ₴. Upsell to people who say
   "I'd rather do it myself" and to everyone who already bought the service.
